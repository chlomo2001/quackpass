QuackPass

Your Ryanair boarding passes, in your browser.
No phone needed.

Privacy policy Source code

What it does

  • Shows the scannable barcode for every checked-in passenger.
  • Saves an Apple Wallet .pkpass, or adds the pass to Google Wallet.
  • Saves a ticket as a sharp PNG, or prints an A4 sheet of wallet-size tickets with cut lines.
  • Downloads a whole booking at once as a ZIP.
  • Shows gate closing, departure and arrival times, and the terminal where there is one.
  • Adds a button to the airline's own booking page, so your passes are one click away.

Nothing is uploaded. QuackPass has no servers and no analytics. It talks to the airline's website using the session you are already logged into, and everything else happens inside your browser.

Install

  • From the Chrome Web Store, once the listing is live.
  • Or load it unpacked: download the source, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.

Good to know

  • You must already be checked in — passes do not exist before then.
  • Some airports and airlines will not accept a printed or screenshotted pass. Check the rules for your route before relying on it.
  • The gate closes 30 minutes before the departure time on your ticket.
